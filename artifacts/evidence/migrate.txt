$SIMD Migration · stopped SIMD Migration Launchpad Holders The migration is stopped Do not send $SIMD anywhere. Do not buy the "Super IMD" token on Robinhood Chain (0x19E4…39F6): it is not supported by SIMD. What happened (Oct 17, 2026) On Oct 17 we opened a migration: holders sent their $SIMD to the migration wallet 0xE132be23CE6255930556a45406075E62fb6340C0 to receive a new token 1:1 on Robinhood Chain. 63 wallets sent 201,151,260 $SIMD to it. That wallet was controlled by the developer, not by the rest of the team. Between 18:31 and 19:18 UTC that wallet sold all 201,151,260 migrated $SIMD (for about 3.4 ETH), sold 736 IMD (about 1.6 ETH), took 2,331 IMD out of the SIMD jobs vault and sent 2,361 IMD to 0xf5C00f19ba97B20d75e19B7B98E87dc71b4aE3A4 . Every one of these transactions is public on Etherscan ↗ . What we're doing The migration is closed and this page no longer lets anyone send tokens. The full list of who sent what is kept below and can be downloaded: it is the record of everyone affected. We're talking with the Identity.md team and the community about what can be done. Updates will be posted on X and in the IMD Telegram.
